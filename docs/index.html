BLE Signal Analyzer

Bluetooth LE Scanner & Signal Meter

BLE Signal Analyzer is a lightweight Android utility for scanning nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices and analyzing their signal strength in real time.

Key features

  • Scan nearby BLE devices
  • Real-time RSSI signal strength
  • Stronger / weaker signal trend
  • Signal history graph
  • Manufacturer and service information
  • Proximity vibration alert
  • No account
  • No ads
  • No cloud
  • No analytics

Simple and privacy-friendly

BLE scanning and signal analysis happen locally on the Android device. The app has no account system, backend, advertising, analytics, or network communication.
